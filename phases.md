The expected project timeline is approximately 2-3 months from signing, receipt of Payment 1, and availability of the required initial inputs. This is an expected planning timeline, not a fixed deadline. The Parties will review progress and next deliverables in regular sync-up calls, expected to be weekly unless decided otherwise.

## Phase 1: Architecture, UX, and Acceptance Criteria

Subspace will define the updated Architecture, UX direction, implementation plan, phase breakdown, and objective acceptance criteria together with Future Invest. Phase 1 will also confirm required design inputs, sample contracts, and the detailed scope boundary for Phases 2-4.

If Phase 1 shows that the Architecture needs changes that do not expand the original scope, Subspace will update the Architecture at no additional cost and implement the adjusted version in later phases.

## Phase 2: Platform Foundation, Portfolio, and Imports

Subspace will implement the core application foundation and main setup workflows needed before quote and contract creation. This includes the Railway deployment foundation, account/user setup, object/property and space setup, template and attachment foundations, audit/notification foundations, and the initial data model for the contract portfolio.

Phase 2 also includes the agreed foundations for contract portfolio import: importing old PDF contracts, AI-assisted extraction/scanning, review of extracted structure, and linking imported real-estate-related agreements to the relevant property, party, or portfolio record. The linked agreement categories include management agreements, maintenance agreements, insurance agreements, and similar supporting agreements.

## Phase 3: Quotes, Portal, Contract Preparation, and AI Foundation

Subspace will implement the quote workflow and the first operator-client loop. This includes the agreed dashboard, quote creation, pricing and VAT handling, quote PDFs, client portal access, client review/response flows, messaging, notifications, and related integrations where required access is available.

Phase 3 also includes the agreed contract-preparation foundation: lease and employment agreement templates, clause and variable foundations, contract draft generation, commenting/review foundations, and contract portfolio views.

The AI agent foundation will also be implemented in Phase 3. This includes the chat interface foundation, Q&A over permitted contract and portfolio context, agent tools for finding and explaining relevant records, and agreed human-in-the-loop assistance workflows described in Annex 3.

## Phase 4: Contract Lifecycle, Signing, Automation, and Pilot

Subspace will complete the end-to-end contract lifecycle functionality for the agreed initial contract types: lease agreements and employment agreements.

Phase 4 includes final contract generation flows, signing, signed-document archive, key dates, indexation where applicable, amendments, audit/export functionality, AI agent production completion, security review, integration validation where credentials are available, pilot support, and handover.
